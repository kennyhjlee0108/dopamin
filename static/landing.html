Features

Everything you need to focus

Tools designed around how you actually think — whether you have ADHD, get easily distracted, or just want to make the most of your study time.

⏱️

Pomodoro Timer

Focus sessions, short breaks, and long breaks with a beautiful ring timer that keeps you on track — no overwhelm, just one session at a time.

🧠

AI Study Friend

A warm, focus-aware AI companion that knows your task list, your energy level, and exactly how to nudge you out of paralysis without judgment.

🎧

Ambient Focus Sounds

Rain, brown noise, and lo-fi music to mask distractions and help your brain settle into a deep focus state — right from inside the app.

📋

Smart Task Breakdown

AI breaks any overwhelming task into 3–5 tiny, do-able micro-steps. The "getting started" wall? Gone.

Calendar & Streaks

See your focus history at a glance, track daily streaks, and add notes to any day. Built-in momentum that makes showing up feel good.

Energy Check-ins

Tell the app how you're feeling before each session. Your AI companion adapts its coaching style to your current energy — not a fixed script.
