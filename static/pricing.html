Frequently asked questions

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Free tier is completely free with no credit card required. You only need to pay when you upgrade to Pro.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — you can cancel your Pro subscription at any time from your account settings. You keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.

Is this really built for ADHD?

Yes. The AI Study Friend's coaching style, the short-session focus philosophy, task breakdown, and energy check-ins are all designed around ADHD research and the challenges that come with ADHD executive function.

What is the AI Study Friend?

It's an ADHD-aware AI companion inside the app. It knows your task list, your current timer state, and your energy level — and gives you personalised nudges, task breakdowns, and celebration when you finish a session.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards via Stripe. Your payment is processed securely and we never store your card details.
